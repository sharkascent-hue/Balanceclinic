Questions, answered.

Does it hurt?
Most people describe a quick warm flick, like an elastic band. The handpiece cools the skin as it works.
Can I shave between sessions?
Yes. Shave the area 24 hours before each session, but avoid waxing or plucking during your course.
How soon will I see results?
Hair sheds 1 to 3 weeks after each session. Regrowth becomes noticeably finer from the third session.
