Polynucleotides for tired eyes

Dark circles and crepey skin under the eye are hard to treat. Polynucleotides change that.

Skin · 5 min read

Drag
Under-eye darkness

The skin under the eye is the thinnest on the body. It loses collagen early, shows every shadow and reacts badly to aggressive treatment. For years the options were concealer or filler.

Repair, not volume

Polynucleotides are purified DNA fragments that signal the skin to repair itself. Injected in a series of tiny deposits, they improve hydration, thickness and elasticity, so darkness lifts and the crepey texture smooths. The LumaDoc protocol we use is designed for this delicate area.

Most clients have two to three sessions, three weeks apart, and see the change build over two months.
